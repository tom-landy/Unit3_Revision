## Making an episode in NotebookLM

1. Create a new notebook for each episode. One focused notebook per episode gives a much tighter podcast than one notebook with everything in it.
2. Add the episode's source pack as the only source (upload the .txt file, or paste its text as copied text).
3. Open Audio Overview, choose Customize, choose the Deep Dive format, and paste the episode's prompt from below.
4. Generate, then listen all the way through before publishing (see the checks below).
5. Download the audio and rename it to the file name listed in `public/data/podcasts.js` (E01.m4a, E02.m4a and so on). Upload it to `public/audio/`. The episode then appears on its topic page and in the Podcasts tab.

## Checks before publishing

- **Numbers.** AI hosts sometimes misread figures or get a calculation wrong. Check every number and formula against the pack. The site tells students to trust the key points if a podcast disagrees.
- **Dated figures.** FSCS limit £120,000 (older papers say £85,000), ISA allowance, contactless limit, payday loan caps. Make sure the hosts don't mix up old and new figures.
- **Made-up rules.** Listen for invented examples presented as fact, e.g. specific interest rates or product names not in the pack.
- **Length.** Short is better for revision. If an episode runs long, regenerate with the shorter length option or narrow the prompt.
